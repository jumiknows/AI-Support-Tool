# Video Clips for Counsellor Avatar

This directory contains video clips that correspond to different stages of the counselling conversation.

## Required Video Files

### Clip A - `a.mp4` (Intro + boundaries)
**Duration:** ~15-20 seconds
**Purpose:** Set tone, build safety, and clearly state it's not real therapy
**Script:**
> Hi, I'm glad you're here. My role is to listen and help you reflect on what's been stressing you out lately.
> I'm an AI-based support tool, not a professional counsellor, so I can't give diagnoses or handle emergencies.
> But we can talk through what's on your mind and look for small ways to make things feel a bit more manageable.

**Visual:** Soft smile, steady eye contact, gentle head nods

---

### Clip B - `b.mp4` (Check-in prompt)
**Duration:** ~10-15 seconds
**Purpose:** Invite them to share a mild stressor
**Script:**
> To start, I'd like to check in.
> When you think about the last few days or weeks, what has been weighing on you the most?
> You can share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with.

**Visual:** Lean forward slightly, open posture, soft curiosity in face

---

### Clip C - `c.mp4` (Gentle follow-up / explore)
**Duration:** ~10-15 seconds
**Purpose:** Use after a first disclosure, to invite a bit more detail
**Script:**
> Thank you for sharing that with me. It sounds important.
> If you're comfortable, could you tell me a little more about what that situation felt like for you, or what made it especially stressful?

**Visual:** Empathetic expression, small nods, eyebrows slightly raised in interest

---

### Clip D - `d.mp4` (Empathic validation)
**Duration:** ~10-15 seconds
**Purpose:** Generic "I hear you" response that fits many situations
**Script:**
> That sounds really tough, and it makes sense that you'd feel that way.
> Many people would find that situation hard to handle.
> It's okay to feel how you're feeling about it.

**Visual:** Concerned but warm face, slow nodding, maybe a small sigh to show shared heaviness
**Note:** Currently not used in the flow, but available for future enhancement

---

### Clip E - `e.mp4` (Coping suggestion)
**Duration:** ~15-20 seconds
**Purpose:** Offer one small, safe, non-clinical strategy
**Script:**
> One small thing that can sometimes help in moments like this is to focus on just one next step.
> That could be taking a short break, writing things down, or reaching out to someone you trust.
> As you think about your situation, what is one gentle, doable step that feels realistic for you?

**Visual:** Encouraging expression, slight hand gesture, then pause as if giving space to think

---

### Clip F - `f.mp4` (Wrap-up / closing)
**Duration:** ~15-20 seconds
**Purpose:** End of the short session
**Script:**
> Thank you for taking the time to talk this through.
> You've done something important by putting your thoughts into words.
> As you go back to your day, try to be a bit kind to yourself, the way you would be to a friend in a similar situation.
> And remember, if things ever feel too heavy, it's always okay to reach out to a real person for support.

**Visual:** Warm smile, gentle nod, posture relaxes slightly

---

## Conversation Flow

1. **INTRO** → Clip A (used once at start)
2. **CHECK_IN** → Clip B (start of session)
3. **EXPLORE** → Clip C (gentle follow-up)
4. **COPING** → Clip E (coping suggestion)
5. **WRAP_UP** → Clip F (closing)
6. **END** → Clip F (closing)

## Technical Notes

- Videos should maintain consistent framing and background
- Lighting and audio quality should be professional
- File format: MP4 (H.264)
- Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or 1280x720
- Each clip should feel natural and conversational
- Videos play once when the conversation step changes
- Since all clips contain speech, there is no separate "silent listening" clip
